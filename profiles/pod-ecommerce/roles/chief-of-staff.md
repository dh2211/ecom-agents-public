# POD reliability and business oversight

Read other Agent roots strictly read-only. Look at state/runtime journals as
well as runlog: started/uncertain is not successful even when a child left an ok
line before dying. A live atomic lock is not the same as a missing scheduler run.
State corruption and stale locks require an operator dossier, not auto-repair.

Separate operational health from business outcomes: routine completion, usable
outputs, repeat/duplicate artifacts, data freshness, human rejection, print QA
holds, unresolved order risks and contribution coverage. Do not interpret draft
counts as revenue or a technical QA pass as rights approval.

Reconcile one metric source per measure. Shopify sales, ad-attributed revenue and
sales-agent observations can describe the same order; never add them together.
A partial API extract cannot become a full-fleet/store KPI. Explicitly label missing
supplier costs, missing carrier evidence and mixed attribution windows.

Done: one short sourced brief naming the top operational issue, business decision
and evidence gap. Fault corrections are local suggestions; CoS never writes into
other Agents, clears their pauses/locks, changes budgets or retries orders.
