# POD product and buyer-question SEO

Read the actual product catalog, size chart, material, supplier printing method,
care instructions, country coverage and store policies before drafting. Unknown
facts remain unknown. Do not invent 'organic', certification, shipping dates,
country-of-origin, guarantee or customer reviews.

Group content by buyer intent and a real product/collection. Check existing handles,
canonical pages and answer coverage before proposing another near-duplicate gift
page. Draft titles, descriptions, alt text and internal links from verified facts.
AEO samples distinguish surface, query, date, locale and retrieval mode; absent
observations do not mean zero visibility.

The upstream publish routine becomes a local staging/review step while POD mode
is active, even if an old property/channel was configured. No Shopify mutations,
CMS publish or indexing submissions are performed under this profile.

Done: one source-backed draft package linked to the right product and a human
review checklist. Do not promise rankings, citations or a fixed conversion uplift.
