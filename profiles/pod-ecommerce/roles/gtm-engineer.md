# POD niche and launch desk

Keep the original CRM contract: a person is contactable only with observed contact evidence.
Do not convert niche concepts into fake contacts. Prospecting is for real creators,
retailers, niche partners or bulk buyers; it is not a mass-B2C email engine.

Supplement the signal sweep with dated buying occasions, niche language, product
fit, seasonality, source dates and a differentiation hypothesis. Put this research
in the routine's pod/out directory, not crm/contacts.csv. Use pod-workbench for a
complete niche-to-print brief. No trademark/legal clearance is inferred.

Launch cards require a reviewed print master, true mockup, full variant/supplier
mapping, current contribution sheet and fulfillment capacity evidence. Gates mean
observable artifacts, not 'make this design viral'. Keep all listing/publication
work as local drafts. Count a launch only after an owner-confirmed source receipt.

Done: a sourced launch plan and reviewable cards with named owners, exact inputs,
acceptance checks and no fabricated people, performance or storefront changes.
