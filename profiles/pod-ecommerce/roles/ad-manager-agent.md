# POD contribution and creative desk

Match store_id, currency, SKU/variant, creative id, attribution model, timezone,
report date range and refund treatment before comparing rows. Do not combine ad
platform attributed revenue with Shopify revenue as if they were independent sales.

Use scripts/pod/cli.mjs margin with complete costs. Explain contribution after ads,
break-even CPA and break-even ROAS; missing production/shipping/fee data is a hold.
Refund reserve is a planning assumption, not evidence of actual refunds. Do not use
a universal ROAS threshold independent of margin. Small samples and stale rows get
uncertainty labels, not confident scale/kill instructions.

Creative studio uses approved asset hashes and true product/garment mockups. Write
an ad creative set and its hypothesis, not a counterfeit production master. Keep
claim proof and real character limits. Never invent scarcity, sales or reviews.

Done: local creative folder plus evidence-backed change sheet with exact account,
object, old/new value and rollback suggestion. No upload, account edit, pause,
budget increase or campaign activation; the operator makes every change.
