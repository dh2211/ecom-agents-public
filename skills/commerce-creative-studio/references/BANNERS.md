# Banner creative production

## Inputs

Use an exact product/variant photo, rights evidence, brand colors, audience, angle, offer,
landing-page facts, CTA, placement and language. Separate product imagery from background
inspiration. A supplier-provided photo is not automatically licensed for paid advertising.

## Starting concepts

1. Product hero + one factual benefit: works when the product is visually understandable.
2. Problem -> demonstration: show an honest use case, not fabricated performance evidence.
3. Feature detail: one meaningful detail with a readable callout and source reference.
4. Objection answer: size, contents, material or compatibility, only when documented.
5. Bundle/offer: exact items and current price, no fake crossed-out price or false scarcity.
6. Real social proof: only a licensed, authentic testimonial tied to this product; otherwise omit.

These are hypotheses for testing, not predictions of winning ads.

## Production

Prefer a real product image composited over a controlled layout. For AI scenes, give the
image tool the approved references and lock the variant, shape, label, color, included
accessories and scale. Do not use a generated image as evidence of a feature.
Keep the headline short, contrast high, one focal product and one CTA. Inspect at phone size.
The local helper renders 1:1 (1080x1080), 4:5 (1080x1350) and 9:16 (1080x1920) SVG drafts,
not final platform uploads. It embeds a PNG/JPEG in full without fetching external content.
It creates layout templates, not new AI product photography. Export to PNG/JPEG separately.

The aspect ratios are working presets. Verify the current requirements and UI safe zones
for the exact placement, country and ad type; never assume one safe-zone margin fits all.
Design each composition rather than blindly center-cropping a square into a vertical story.
For a 9:16 ad, relocate text away from the platform's controls using its current overlay.

## Banner QA

Check spelling and diacritics, visible offer/currency, product fidelity, included items,
claim evidence, disclosure, mobile legibility, safe zones, licensed fonts/images and landing
consistency. SVG text is escaped, but that is not visual QA. Wide glyphs and long translations
can still require manual re-layout. Inspect the exported raster, not only the SVG preview.

Freeze the approved source references and final hash. Changing the headline, price, image
or crop creates a new revision and needs a new review. A previously approved creative is
not a license to publish a different export.
