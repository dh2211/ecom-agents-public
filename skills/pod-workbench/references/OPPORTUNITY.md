# Evidence-backed opportunity brief

Inputs: explicit niche, geography/language, buyer occasion, product/blank, sources,
brand constraints and existing concepts. Geography is market context, not an
inference about a person's name or appearance.

Output: dated source log; observed pattern vs hypothesis; 3 differentiated original
concepts; exact proposed design text; product/placement; color/contrast plan;
source-rights review items; unknowns; one testable acceptance check per concept.
Do not copy competitor artwork or assert that a public image is licensed for resale.
No logo, character, celebrity likeness or protected slogan is cleared automatically.
A rights review is a human decision, not a guarantee supplied by this skill.

Acceptance: another designer can produce the print master from the brief without
inventing its wording, supplier dimensions or intended audience. A source count
is not a demand estimate; unsupported numerical claims must be removed.
